Jett's Archive

A private photo and video journal that keeps everything on your own device.

What it does

Jett's Archive lets you keep photos, videos and written journal entries in one place. There is no account to create and no server behind the app. Your journal is stored on your phone or computer.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you switch it on, the app copies your photos, videos and journal into a folder in your own Google Drive, so you have a second copy. You choose the folder, and you can turn the backup off at any time.

  • It is off by default.
  • It uses Google's drive.file permission, so the app can only see files and folders it created itself, never the rest of your Drive.
  • Nothing is sent while you are offline.
