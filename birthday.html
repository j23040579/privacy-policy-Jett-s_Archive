Here's your gift: Jett's Archive. Pick your device below to install it.

1. Android

For your phone or tablet.

  1. Tap the button below to download the APK file.
  2. When it finishes, open the downloaded file (from the notification or your Downloads folder).
  3. If Android asks, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
Download for Android (APK)

If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway. The app isn't on the Play Store, so this is normal.

2. Windows

For your Windows PC (64-bit).

Download for Windows (ZIP)

Unzip it

  1. Open your Downloads folder, right-click photograph-windows-x64.zip and choose Extract All…, then Extract.
  2. Open the new photograph-windows-x64 folder. Keep every file in it together, and don't run the app from inside the zip.
  3. Double-click photograph.exe to start. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.

Add a shortcut to your Desktop

  1. In the folder, right-click photograph.exe.
  2. On Windows 11, choose Show more options (or press Shift+F10).
  3. Choose Send to → Desktop (create shortcut).
  4. A photograph.exe - Shortcut icon now appears on your Desktop. Rename it to Jett's Archive if you like, and double-click it any time to open the app.

Don't move or delete the original folder. The shortcut points to it.
