Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Jett's Archive ("the app") is a personal photo and video journal. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information the app handles

The app stores the photos, videos, captions, journal entries and suggestions that you add. All of it is saved on your own device. The app has no servers, no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and we do not receive, collect or see your content.

Optional Google Drive backup

The backup is off by default. If you turn it on, you sign in with your Google Account and the app asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the app can create and update only the files and folders that it created itself in your Google Drive. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

When the backup is on, the app sends the following to your Google Drive, directly from your device:

  • your photos and videos;
  • your journal data (a file called journal.json).

This data goes to the folder you choose in your own Drive and nowhere else. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

Sign-in information

After you sign in, Google gives the app a token that lets it keep backing up without asking you to sign in every time. This token is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. It is never sent to us.

How we use Google user data

The app uses data from your Google Account only to provide the backup feature you asked for: copying your own content into your own Drive. It does not use this data for advertising, does not sell it, does not share it, and no person reads it.

Jett's Archive's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn the backup off in the app at any time. Nothing more is sent.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your backed-up copies by deleting the folder in Google Drive. Deleting an item inside the app does not delete its copy from Drive.
  • Delete the data on your device by deleting items in the app or uninstalling it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
